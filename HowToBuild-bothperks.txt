BothPerks build workflow
========================

Paths are configured in:

  ModPaths.local.psd1

Current version families:

  v1_2 -> D:\BANNERLORDS\Mount & Blade II Bannerlord
  v1_3 -> D:\BANNERLORDS\Mount & Blade II Bannerlord1.3.15

Build this mod:

  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\BuildBothPerks.ps1

Runtime layout:

  BothPerks.Bootstrap.dll
  compat\v1_2\BothPerks.Core.dll
  compat\v1_3\BothPerks.Core.dll

The bootstrap chooses v1_2 for Bannerlord 1.2.x and v1_3 for 1.3+.

Clean Workshop release:

  From the parent mods folder:
  powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\BuildReleases.ps1 -ModName BothPerks

Upload from:

  ..\..\releases\BothPerks
